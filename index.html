The gate (live)

    What this is. The build reads only your skin tokens (--w-bg, --w-ink, --w-accent, --w-radius…) — recolour, restyle, reshape it freely. The gate runs the mutation-tested designGate kernel (26/26) against the build's contract every time you change something: is the kernel intact, is every wired control still present, is every required variable defined? Flip the "break it" toggle to watch it reject a redesign that drops a control. All in konomi: the design contribution is witnessed exactly like code — you can iterate endlessly and never ship a broken build.

    MIT · source & mutation gate — the gate is the gated kernel.mjs verbatim (injected, CI-diffed). A designer forks a build, edits only the skin, and the gate clears their PR. Pure client-side.
